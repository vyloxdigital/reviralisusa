Why 41 Ingredients?

Wholebody Wellness Is Not a Single-Ingredient Problem

Daily stress, depleted micronutrients, a compromised immune system and accelerated cellular wear are four distinct issues. Reviralis Complete addresses all four in one daily capsule through its proprietary 4-step Daily Balance Reset Method.

🧠

Step 1 — Stop the Trigger

The modern stress response evolved for short bursts of danger — not 8-hour workdays, constant connectivity and financial pressure. Reviralis begins by targeting the physiological stress cascade before it compounds, using ingredients clinically studied for their adaptogenic and neuromodulatory effects.

Key compounds: Bacopa Monnieri, Phosphatidylserine, Magnesium, DHA (Omega-3), N-Acetyl L-Tyrosine, DMAE Bitartrate

⚡

Step 2 — Support the Wear

Chronic activation of the stress response depletes key B-vitamins, magnesium and electrolytes faster than a typical diet replaces them. Step 2 replenishes these critical cofactors to restore the metabolic pathways that produce and regulate cellular energy.

Key compounds: Vitamin B1, B2, B3, B5, B6, B12, Folate, Biotin, Choline, Calcium, Potassium, Cinnamon Bark Extract

🛡️

Step 3 — Seal the Exit Points

The immune system is the body's primary defense network, and chronic stress measurably impairs it. Step 3 targets immune resilience through a combination of antioxidants, vitamins and botanical extracts that support the body's natural barriers and innate defenses.

Key compounds: Vitamin C, D, A, E, Zinc, Selenium, Green Tea (EGCG), Grape Seed Extract, Olive Leaf Extract, Garlic Extract

🔬

Step 4 — Future-Proof Your Body

Cellular vitality, metabolic balance and long-term resilience require trace minerals and targeted botanicals that most people never consume in sufficient amounts. Step 4 addresses this gap to support the body's deep homeostatic mechanisms.

Key compounds: Chromium, Bilberry Extract, Inositol, Glutamic Acid, Boron, Vanadyl Sulfate, Molybdenum, Manganese, Iodine, Copper
